## Complete an order

1. **Read the brief and check capacity.** Review the age/state list, contact details, 1–8 services and USD prices, featured service, colours and notes. Keep no more than three active paid orders; count them manually and pause intake at the limit. Check scope and information directly with the customer. Agree timing individually and save approved content and the confirmed timing before requesting payment. A submitted brief alone is not a confirmed order or production start.
2. **Send payment instructions manually.** Copy the order-specific Paynow link and reference. The price is US$20. Ask the customer to confirm USD at Paynow checkout. For another currency, quote explicitly; never infer an exchange rate.
3. **Verify the actual transaction in Paynow.** Check transaction ID, order reference, USD currency, gross amount and **Paid** status in the merchant account. Enter the provider transaction ID and actual fees; the form starts pending and does not assume zero fees. A screenshot, customer message or returned browser page is insufficient. Keep ambiguous or mismatched payments pending. A provider transaction ID cannot pay two orders.
4. **Record finances separately.** Enter actual fees from Paynow and verify the calculated net amount (gross minus fees) against Paynow; do not guess fees. Record settlement separately when it really reaches the destination. Payment confirmation is not settlement. Financial exports and totals distinguish these fields and exclude test orders.
5. **Generate the current approved content.** Choose a preset and, if supplied with permission, load a validated PNG or JPEG logo locally (maximum 4 MB, 4096 pixels per side). SVG/executable files are not accepted. Save a new output version after adding, replacing or removing a logo. Only its SHA-256 fingerprint is stored: after refresh/order switching, reselect the matching local file before regenerating. The generator does not upload the logo. Production is blocked until verified payment and scope confirmation.
6. **Inspect all four outputs.** The desk shows all four images; the A4 preview is the exact artwork embedded in the PDF and links to the PDF itself. Open the A4 PDF and all three 1080 × 1920 PNGs: services/prices, featured service/offer, and business/booking contact. Check spelling, USD values, facts, contact number, logo, contrast, wrapping and safe margins. Resolve any fit error. Download the versioned files together.
7. **Review, send drafts and handle the revision.** Approve only the inspected current version. Send the drafts through WhatsApp, confirm when the customer received them and record that receipt date and the seven-calendar-day revision deadline in notes. The customer may send one consolidated change list through WhatsApp within that window; check it manually before using the included revision once. Correct operator mistakes without consuming that revision. Changing approved content creates a new output version and clears the earlier review approval; regenerate and inspect it. Delivery recording is blocked for unreviewed or stale outputs.
8. **Send the final files manually through WhatsApp.** Only after actual delivery record the delivery action and useful channel/time notes. Generation or a ZIP download is not delivery. Retain the sent version privately under the 30-day design-file policy below. If the included revision is requested after first delivery, create, inspect and deliver its new version through the same gates.

## Payment-link behavior

The configured recipient comes from the owner's supplied link. Per-order links follow Paynow's documented sequence: URL-encode values, join `search`, `amount=20.00`, `reference` and `l=1`, Base64-encode that string, then URL-encode the result into `q`. There is no invented currency parameter. Even locked simple links can be edited; the actual transaction must be checked. See [Paynow's simple payment documentation](https://developers.paynow.co.zw/docs/paynow/simple_paynow_request_button/).

## Capacity, support and corrections

The approved limit is three active paid orders, including paid work still awaiting a revision or final delivery. Count these manually before accepting another payment, pause new intake from the desk at the limit and reopen when a slot is available. Existing records remain available for fulfilment. The application does not automatically count against or enforce this limit. Contact routes, Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00 CAT (Africa/Harare) response hours and the unpaid cleanup cutoff are configured in the production Worker settings. Confirm timing individually after reviewing each brief; deliver through WhatsApp.

Use notes to record agreed timing, customer approval, first-draft receipt and revision deadline, the consolidated change list, unusual payment facts, delivery channel and any cancellation/refund enquiry. Do not record card details, PINs or passwords. Consider cancellation/refund requests case by case, communicate the decision and process any approved refund personally. Nothing in this build moves funds. The seven-calendar-day revision window begins when the customer receives the first drafts, not when files are generated or merely sent; the application does not calculate or enforce that deadline.

If another tab changes an order, your draft remains visible but cannot overwrite the newer saved content. Refresh the queue, compare the changes, then use **Reload latest saved content** to replace your draft before continuing. Generation uses saved approved content, not unsaved edits. A page refresh loses browser-local generated files; regenerating clears the prior review approval. A plain refresh does not change the stored review state.

The owner has authorized one fictional production rehearsal and opening for customers only after every launch check passes. Verify owner sign-in and access boundaries using the configured Access application first; keep intake paused during configuration and anonymous checks. Open only for the fictional submission and necessary live Turnstile acceptance/replay assertions, then pause again immediately. Mark the record **fictional/test** before entering any simulated verification. That flag is permanent and excludes the record from live totals. Use explicitly simulated transaction, settlement and delivery notes and never treat them as actual transactions. Local development marks test records automatically. The rehearsal does not establish actual Paynow USD/merchant verification; that blocker must also be resolved before customer intake opens. A failed or incomplete launch check keeps customer intake paused.

## Records and retention

Export order and financial records regularly and before destructive maintenance. CSV/JSON exports are private customer records. The owner-selected private backup folder and its three child folders have been created and verified; consult the ignored `.wrangler/owner-operations.json` for the actual path. Keep financial records separate from short-lived design assets, check that exported files open correctly and never commit them or the private path. Keep test orders marked as tests and confirm they stay outside live submitted/paid/revenue/delivery totals.

The approved unpaid cleanup cutoff is 90 days; set `RETENTION_DAYS` to `90` (supported range: 30–3650). Cleanup removes only unpaid orders with no provider transaction ID whose last update is older than that cutoff. It is measured from the last saved update, not necessarily the submission date. There is no automatic deletion job.

Paid orders remain preserved in D1 for manual retention review. Required paid business/tax records must be kept for at least six years from the last entry under section 37B of the Income Tax Act, and longer where applicable requirements or a legal hold require it; see [ZIMRA's record-keeping guidance](https://www.zimra.co.zw/domestic-taxes/corporate/record-keeping-explained). Do not interpret this as automatic deletion six years after payment. The application currently retains the whole paid order, including the brief and contact data; it does not extract financial-only records or delete paid orders on a schedule.

Export private JSON first and inspect the records older than the configured cutoff. After confirming they are no longer needed, use **Retention cleanup** below the desk, select the confirmation checkbox and remove eligible unpaid briefs. The equivalent authenticated API command, from browser developer tools while signed into `/admin`, is:

```javascript
await fetch('/api/admin/retention', {
  method: 'POST',
  headers: { 'Content-Type': 'application/json' },
  body: JSON.stringify({ confirmed: true })
}).then(response => response.json());
```

This deletes eligible database records immediately. Check the returned count and keep the export private. Changing `RETENTION_DAYS` changes eligibility; it does not run cleanup by itself.

Manually remove downloaded design files and customer logos 30 days after final delivery, except evidence required for an applicable legal or unresolved-dispute requirement. Record the final delivery date and review the corresponding local folders and design-file backup copies when the cutoff arrives. This period does not apply to required financial records or exports; keep those separately for the paid-record retention period. The browser does not upload logos, so only their fingerprints are stored in the order record.

Review WhatsApp conversations, backups and Paynow records separately. D1 cleanup does not remove any of these or local downloads. There is no automatic 30-day file cleanup, seven-day revision deadline or three-order capacity control; the operator must perform and record these checks.
